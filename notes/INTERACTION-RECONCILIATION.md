# Interaction scope

The supported inventory lives in README. Standard AHP send/cancel/approve/rename and
paged navigation share the host's public behavior. Agent access to terminal-local
state uses explicitly targeted client tools; renderer revision checks reject edits
racing unflushed keystrokes. Draft inspection is explicit, never a background mirror.
Unknown client effects stay in the local journal and are never replayed. Rich device,
queue, steer, takeover and advanced configuration remain unavailable until negotiated
and mapped through independently owned capability providers.
